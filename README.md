## What I do

I'm a **Generative AI Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services**, building and shipping AI systems used by millions of users. My work lives at the intersection of agentic pipelines, LLM safety, and production observability — not just prototypes, but systems that run in the real world.

- **Multi-agent orchestration** using LangGraph and LangChain — designing agent-to-agent communication, tool selection, and execution planning
- **LLMOps & observability** — instrumenting 35+ LLM/ML metrics via OpenTelemetry, Jaeger, MLflow, LangKit, and Evidently AI for drift detection and real-time tracing
- **LLM safety & guardrails** — modular pipelines for PII masking, toxicity detection, bias filtering, and prompt injection defense across enterprise deployments
- **RAG at scale** — hierarchical retrieval systems with 100,000+ clustered embeddings, tuned for hallucination resistance under <3s latency
- **MCP integration** — building tool-augmented agents that connect LLMs to real-world APIs and data sources
